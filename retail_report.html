Key Findings
What the Data Tells Us
📈
Technology is the star category
Highest sales ($836K) and highest profit ($145K). Every dollar invested in technology yields the best return.
🪑
Furniture barely breaks even
Despite $742K in sales, furniture generates only $18K profit — a wafer-thin 2.5% margin. Pricing is broken here.
🌎
West region outperforms all others
West leads in both sales ($725K) and profit ($108K). Central region lags significantly — worth investigating.
🏷️
Heavy discounts are burning money
Orders with 40%+ discounts lose an average of $107 each. This single issue could be costing hundreds of thousands annually.
🖨️
One product earns $25K alone
The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier generates $25,199 in profit — more than 3× the next best product.
🎄
Q4 is peak season every year
November 2017 hit $118K in sales — the highest month. Sales consistently surge in Sep–Nov every year.

Action Plan
Recommendations for the Business
1
Cap all discounts at 20% immediately. Discounts above 20% generate losses on every single order. This is the fastest way to improve profit margin without changing anything else.
2
Audit Furniture pricing and discount strategy. With only 2.5% profit margin on $742K of sales, furniture needs urgent review of pricing, supplier costs, and discounting habits.
3
Double down on Technology products. Highest revenue and highest profit — prioritize inventory stocking, marketing budget, and sales team focus here.
4
Expand in the West region. The West already leads in both sales and profit. More marketing investment and regional sales staff will compound returns further.
5
Prepare for Q4 demand surge every year. November consistently spikes. Stock up on top-selling Technology products by September to avoid lost sales from stockouts.
